# Life Cycle Economies

Python implementation of the life-cycle model in:

> Storesletten, K., Telmer, C. I., and Yaron, A. (2004).  
> *Consumption and Risk Sharing over the Life Cycle*.  
> Journal of Monetary Economics, 51(3), 609–633.

The notebook solves a heterogeneous-agent life-cycle economy with incomplete markets, idiosyncratic earnings risk, retirement, survival risk, borrowing constraints, and precautionary savings.

## Contents

- Endogenous Grid Method (EGM) solution;
- stationary equilibrium;
- consumption and income profiles by age;
- alternative pension replacement rates;
- cross-sectional dispersion of log earnings and consumption.
